Regulatory research

A public CDSCO reference worth verifying.

An official CDSCO-hosted publication includes a Macsur-named entry in its WHO-GMP manufacturing-unit data. We are preserving the exact scope and source context rather than turning it into a broad or current certification claim.

CDSCO WHO-GMP data entry

Listed nameM/s. Macsur Pharma India Limited
Listed locationR.S. No. 243, Thirukanchi Road, Othiyampet, Villianur, Puducherry – 605110
Product forms shownTablets, capsules and powders (non beta-lactam)
The CDSCO-hosted PDF is titled “WHO-GMP Certified Manufacturing Units for Certificate of Pharmaceutical Products (COPPs) in various states of India” and its relevant table is labelled Jan 2023 to Mar 2024. The entry is a valuable historical public reference, but it does not by itself establish that a certificate is valid today, confirm its current scope, or prove present production activity. Obtain the current certificate, issuing authority, certificate number, scope and expiry before making a live certification claim.
