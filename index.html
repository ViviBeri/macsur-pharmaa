About the company

A pharmaceutical manufacturer rooted in Puducherry.

Macsur Pharmaa (India) Private Limited is a privately held company incorporated on 25 May 2006 and registered with the Registrar of Companies, Pondicherry.

Public company and GST records classify its business in pharmaceutical and medicinal product manufacturing. Its registered address is on Thirukanchi Road, Othiyampet, Villianur Commune, Puducherry.
