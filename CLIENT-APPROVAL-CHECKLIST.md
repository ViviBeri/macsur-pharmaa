# Macsur Pharmaa — Client Approval Checklist

Use this checklist in the client meeting before turning the demonstration into the company's official website.

## 1. Corporate identity

- [ ] Confirm the exact legal company name and current CIN.
- [ ] Confirm registered office and actual manufacturing/facility address.
- [ ] Confirm the year of incorporation and approved company description.
- [ ] Confirm the official email, phone number and enquiry contact person.
- [ ] Approve the company logo, brand colours and preferred spelling of the brand.

## 2. Manufacturing and products

- [ ] Confirm whether the company currently manufactures tablets, capsules, powders or other dosage forms.
- [ ] Obtain the approved product/category list and permitted public descriptions.
- [ ] Confirm which manufacturing services may be advertised (own manufacturing, contract manufacturing, loan licence, third-party manufacturing, etc.).
- [ ] Obtain verified facility details and capacity figures only if the company approves publication.
- [ ] Confirm whether export markets, clients or partners may be named.

## 3. Quality and regulatory evidence

- [ ] Obtain current manufacturing licences and confirm their scope and validity.
- [ ] Obtain current GMP/WHO-GMP/ISO or other certificates, if applicable, and confirm issuer, site and expiry.
- [ ] Obtain the approved quality policy and quality-system description.
- [ ] Confirm whether any regulatory approvals or registrations may be displayed publicly.
- [ ] Do not publish expired, historical, draft or out-of-scope documents as current credentials.

## 4. Photography and brand assets

- [ ] Company logo in SVG or high-resolution transparent PNG.
- [ ] Exterior and entrance photographs of the actual facility.
- [ ] Approved production-area and cleanroom photographs.
- [ ] Approved quality-control laboratory and equipment photographs.
- [ ] Product/packaging photographs with permission to publish.
- [ ] Written confirmation that the company owns or is licensed to use supplied images.

## 5. Website functionality

- [ ] Confirm whether the enquiry form may open an email app or must submit directly to a company mailbox/CRM.
- [ ] Confirm who receives business enquiries and the expected response process.
- [ ] Approve the contact address, map pin, email and telephone links.
- [ ] Approve privacy notice and any cookie/analytics requirements if tracking is added.
- [ ] Approve page title, search description and social sharing preview.

## 6. Final sign-off

- [ ] Company representative has reviewed all copy and imagery.
- [ ] All legal, manufacturing and regulatory claims have documentary support.
- [ ] Mobile and desktop versions have been reviewed.
- [ ] Final domain and hosting ownership have been agreed.
- [ ] Written approval received before replacing the demo label with an official launch.

**Current demo limitation:** the website is a static concept. Its enquiry form uses `mailto:` and does not store or transmit submitted data to a backend.
